## zelda-bracelet

Prepared artwork height: 18 pixels.

Input images: `zelda-bracelet-closeup.png`, `swords-v2-source.png`.

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: one isolated 8-bit-inspired fantasy game item sprite on a genuinely transparent background. Subject: the bracelet Zelda gives Impa in Skyward Sword. Image 1 is a close-up in-game screenshot of the EXACT bracelet; use ONLY the bracelet itself as SHAPE AND COLOR reference, remove all the dark background and surrounding scene. Image 2 is a pixel-art sword sprite sheet: STYLE reference ONLY, do not draw swords. Redraw the small bracelet as an openwork bluish-purple wrist cuff, three slim parallel ring bands joined by angular crossing diagonal struts, creating large transparent triangular and diamond-shaped openings between them. The top has an open oval wrist hole. Preserve the reference's tilted three-quarter view, with its cylindrical length leaning down to the right. Palette: periwinkle blue, lavender purple, pale cool ivory highlight pixels, darker violet underside. No gold, gemstone, pendant, clasp, chain, eye symbol, hands or person. A simple elegant cage-like cuff, NOT a solid broad band. Match crisp square pixels, stepped edges and limited shading of the sword style. Readable at a final size about 18 pixels tall, so make the frame and openings clear and chunky. Entire object centered and fully visible with broad transparent margins; no external glow, mist, shadows, floor, text or watermark.
