# Skyward Sword backdrop references

The scenery in `script-v2.html` remains code-drawn pixel art. Its editable source is `tools/location-backdrops.js`; run `node tools/install-backdrops.cjs` after editing it. Actor positions and the 170 × 105 background grid are unchanged.

- [Nintendo: Sealed Grounds](https://zelda.nintendo.com/skyward-sword-hd/world/) — grassy descending terraces and central seal, rather than an ordinary forest.
- [Sealed Grounds and temple architecture](https://www.architectureofzelda.com/the-sealed-grounds-and-intentions-of-the-goddess.html) — heavy piers, recessed stonework, winged crest, green-grey stone and roof light. Past and present share the architecture; the present has moss, cracks and muted stone.
- [Lanayru Gorge screenshot](https://zeldawiki.wiki/wiki/Lanayru_Gorge) — flat-topped ochre cliffs, a deep chasm, circular industrial platform and sparse cacti. Separate from the desert Temple of Time; the Timeshift field restores green ground locally.
- [Lanayru's ancient architecture](https://www.architectureofzelda.com/) — blue-green masonry, circular ornament and industrial motifs inform the stylized Temple of Time courtyard.
- [Skyview Spring screenshot](https://www.architectureofzelda.com/uploads/3/7/1/2/37126503/9413862_orig.png) — green pool, narrow waterfalls and a raised goddess shrine. Used for the sacred-springs scene.
- [Skyloft screenshots](https://www.nintendolife.com/games/wii/legend_of_zelda_skyward_sword/screenshots) — floating rock islands, village roofs, windmill, waterfall and a continuous sea of clouds.

The ancient surface is an interpretive reconstruction using the temple's architectural vocabulary. Demise's realm uses an open reflective water plane and storm sky. These are simplified stage backdrops, not exact camera-view reproductions.
